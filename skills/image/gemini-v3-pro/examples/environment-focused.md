# Environment-Focused Examples - Gemini 3 Pro

This file contains examples optimized for creating compelling locations, atmospheres, and environmental scenes with rich detail.

---

## Example 1: Interior Space - Coffee Shop Morning

### Scenario
Establishing shot for a commercial showcasing café atmosphere.

### Prompt
```
Generate a photorealistic wide shot of a cozy neighborhood coffee shop interior on a 
bright morning. The space features exposed brick walls painted white, reclaimed wood 
flooring with a warm honey finish, and high ceilings with black industrial pendant 
lights hanging at varying heights. Large street-facing windows flood the space with 
soft morning sunlight, creating long shadows across scattered bistro tables with 
mismatched vintage chairs. A long wooden counter with brass espresso machine gleams 
in the natural light. Potted plants—fiddle leaf figs and hanging pothos—add organic 
greenery. A few customers sit at tables working on laptops, their forms slightly out 
of focus in the background. Shot with 24mm wide-angle lens for spacious feel, capturing 
depth and layers. 16:9 landscape orientation. The atmosphere is warm, welcoming, and 
quietly productive. Morning light quality with soft color temperature around 5000K.
```

### Configuration Notes
- **Resolution:** 2K for initial review, 4K for final marketing materials
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (horizontal, good for establishing shots)
- **Key Elements:** Natural light, architectural details, atmospheric depth

## Example 2: Exterior - Urban Alley at Dusk

### Scenario
Location establishing shot for urban narrative scene.

### Prompt
```
Create a cinematic photograph of a narrow urban alley at dusk in a modern city. The 
alley is lined with brick buildings three to four stories tall, their walls covered 
in layers of old concert posters and street art. Warm Edison bulb string lights hang 
overhead, creating pools of amber light against the deepening blue twilight sky. A 
fire escape zigzags up the left building, its black metal silhouette creating graphic 
lines. Wet pavement reflects the lights and the last glow of sunset visible at the 
alley's far end. Small restaurant back doors with glowing interior lights visible. 
Steam rises from a street grate. The ground shows puddles from recent rain, reflecting 
the color-rich scene above. Shot with 35mm lens at f/2.8 for balanced focus throughout 
the depth of the alley. 21:9 ultrawide cinematic ratio. The mood is atmospheric, 
slightly melancholic, urban poetry. Color palette: deep blues, warm amber, rich blacks.
```

### Configuration Notes
- **Resolution:** 4K (cinematic quality for narrative work)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 21:9 (cinematic ultrawide)
- **Lighting:** "Magic hour" transition light with practical lights

## Example 3: Natural Environment - Forest Path Morning

### Scenario
Nature scene for wellness brand content.

### Prompt
```
Illustrate a serene forest path on an early autumn morning. A narrow dirt trail winds 
through tall deciduous trees—oaks and maples—their leaves just beginning to turn gold 
and red, with most foliage still green. Shafts of golden morning sunlight pierce through 
the canopy at dramatic angles, creating visible light rays (god rays) in the misty air. 
The forest floor is covered in a carpet of moss, fallen leaves, and small ferns. A light 
morning mist hovers at knee height, giving the scene an ethereal quality. The path curves 
gently to the right and disappears into soft-focus distance. No people visible, creating 
a sense of peaceful solitude. Shot with 50mm lens at f/4 to maintain focus on the path 
while allowing gentle bokeh in the distance. 9:16 vertical orientation for mobile story 
format. The atmosphere is peaceful, rejuvenating, and quietly spiritual. Color palette: 
warm golden light, rich greens, soft earth tones.
```

### Configuration Notes
- **Resolution:** 2K (optimized for social media)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (vertical/mobile)
- **Key Elements:** Light rays, atmospheric mist, seasonal color

## Environment Lighting Reference

### Time of Day Descriptions

**Golden Hour (Sunrise/Sunset):**
```
Warm golden sunlight at low angle, creating long shadows. Color temperature 3000-4000K. 
Rich oranges, warm yellows, and soft pinks. High contrast between highlights and shadows.
```

**Blue Hour (Pre-dawn/Post-sunset):**
```
Cool diffused light with blue-purple tones. Color temperature 8000-12000K. Even, soft 
illumination with minimal shadows. Artificial lights become prominent against dusky sky.
```

**Midday:**
```
Bright direct sunlight from overhead. Color temperature 5500-6500K. Neutral white light, 
strong shadows directly beneath objects, high contrast, less atmospheric mood.
```

**Overcast Day:**
```
Soft diffused light without harsh shadows. Even illumination across surfaces. Slightly 
cool color temperature. Muted color saturation, perfect for detail work without glare.
```

**Night (Urban):**
```
Mix of artificial light sources: streetlights (warm amber), neon signs (colored), building 
lights (warm yellow). Deep shadows with pools of illumination. High contrast, vibrant colors.
```

## Technical Tips for Environment Shots

### Depth & Layers
Build scenes with foreground, middle ground, and background elements to create visual depth and interest.

### Leading Lines
Use architectural elements, paths, or natural features to guide the eye through the composition.

### Lighting Ratio
Define the relationship between key light and fill light to control mood (high ratio = dramatic, low ratio = even/soft).

### Color Harmony
Choose complementary color schemes (warm vs cool) or analogous palettes (variations within one color family).

### Scale Reference
Include subtle human scale references (furniture, doorways, stairs) to help viewers understand space dimensions.

---

**Pro Tip:** When creating environments for character placement later, slightly underexpose ambient lighting and use neutral-warm color grading. This makes it easier to composite characters naturally and gives flexibility in post-production.
