# Style Variations Examples - Gemini 3 Pro

This file contains examples demonstrating various artistic styles, aesthetics, and visual approaches for different creative needs.

---

## Example 1: Photorealistic Style

### Scenario
Commercial product photography requiring maximum realism.

### Prompt
```
Create a photorealistic product photograph of a premium leather briefcase. The briefcase 
is rich cognac brown leather with visible grain texture, brass hardware with subtle 
patina, and a structured rectangular shape. It sits on a dark walnut wood surface with 
soft reflection visible. The leather shows natural variation in tone and small texture 
details that make it feel real and tactile. Lighting is a classic studio setup: key light 
from upper left at 45 degrees creating dimension, soft fill light from right preventing 
harsh shadows, subtle rim light from behind defining the edge. The background is a gradient 
from charcoal gray to black. Every detail is sharp—stitching, hardware engravings, leather 
texture, wood grain. Shot with 100mm macro lens at f/8 for maximum depth of field. Color 
accurate with perfect white balance. 3:4 portrait orientation. The aesthetic is premium, 
sophisticated, tactile. Commercial product photography with DSLR quality and post-processing 
polish.
```

### Configuration Notes
- **Resolution:** 4K (maximum detail for product work)
- **Key Words:** "photorealistic", "visible grain texture", "tactile", "every detail is sharp"
- **Technical:** Specific lighting setup, macro lens, perfect white balance

## Example 2: Cinematic Film Look

### Scenario
Narrative scene requiring cinematic color grading and composition.

### Prompt
```
Generate a cinematic still of a detective in a film noir style scene. A man in his 40s 
wearing a gray trench coat and fedora sits alone in a dimly lit diner booth at night. 
He stares out the window where neon signs cast colored light (red and blue) across his 
weathered face. A cup of coffee and scattered case files sit on the Formica table. The 
diner interior has warm yellow-orange lights contrasting with the cool blue night visible 
through windows. Venetian blind shadows create dramatic lines. Shot with anamorphic lens 
aesthetic—2.39:1 aspect ratio with characteristic lens flares from the neon lights. Film 
grain visible, giving texture. Color graded with crushed blacks, muted midtones, and 
saturated highlights. Shallow depth of field isolates the detective while background 
blurs into bokeh. The mood is lonely, contemplative, noir. Cinematic feature film aesthetic 
with sophisticated color science. 21:9 ultrawide cinematic ratio.
```

### Configuration Notes
- **Resolution:** 4K (cinematic quality)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 21:9 (widescreen cinema)
- **Key Words:** "cinematic", "film noir", "anamorphic lens", "film grain", "color graded"

## Example 3: Editorial Illustration Style

### Scenario
Magazine article illustration with contemporary graphic approach.

### Prompt
```
Illustrate a modern editorial piece depicting the concept of work-life balance. The 
composition shows a stylized figure split vertically down the center: left side dressed 
in business attire with briefcase against an office background of geometric buildings 
and clock faces; right side in casual clothes with yoga mat against a natural setting 
with plants and flowing water. The figure's pose mirrors on both sides creating symmetry. 
The art style is contemporary digital illustration with clean vector-like shapes, limited 
color palette (navy blue, coral pink, mint green, and cream), and flat shading with 
minimal gradients. Subtle texture overlay gives analog warmth to the digital aesthetic. 
Bold graphic shapes and simplified forms make the concept immediately readable. 3:4 
portrait orientation. The mood is thoughtful, balanced, modern. Editorial illustration 
style similar to contemporary magazine artwork—sophisticated but accessible.
```

### Configuration Notes
- **Resolution:** 2K (optimized for publication)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 3:4 (standard editorial)
- **Key Words:** "editorial illustration", "vector-like", "limited color palette", "flat shading"

## Example 4: Vintage Film Photography

### Scenario
Nostalgic aesthetic for lifestyle brand with retro appeal.

### Prompt
```
Create a photograph that looks like it was shot on vintage 35mm film in the 1970s. A 
young couple sits on the hood of a classic car at a drive-in movie theater at dusk. 
They wear period-appropriate clothing—he in a brown suede jacket, she in a floral maxi 
dress. The image has all the characteristics of 1970s color film: slightly faded colors 
with warm yellow-orange cast, soft focus with glow in highlights, visible grain throughout, 
slight vignetting in corners, and the characteristic color palette of Kodachrome film. 
Light leaks create ethereal flares in the upper corner. The colors lean warm with muted 
saturation—avocado greens, burnt oranges, golden yellows, and chocolate browns. The 
composition is slightly off-center and casual, like a candid moment. 4:5 portrait orientation. 
The mood is nostalgic, romantic, carefree. Vintage analog film photography aesthetic with 
authentic period feel.
```

### Configuration Notes
- **Resolution:** 2K (period-appropriate resolution)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 (classic film format)
- **Key Words:** "vintage 35mm film", "1970s", "Kodachrome", "grain", "light leaks", "faded colors"

## Example 5: Minimalist Design

### Scenario
Brand identity work requiring clean, minimal aesthetic.

### Prompt
```
Generate a minimalist product composition featuring a skincare bottle. A single frosted 
glass bottle with clean sans-serif black label sits centered on a pure white surface 
against a pure white background. The bottle casts a subtle soft shadow. One delicate 
green eucalyptus leaf is placed artfully beside the bottle, providing the only accent 
of color and organic form. The lighting is perfectly even and diffused, creating no harsh 
shadows—just gentle dimensional modeling. The composition is perfectly centered with 
generous negative space around the subject. Every line is clean and precise. The color 
palette is restricted: white, frosted glass transparency, black text, and muted green. 
The aesthetic is about restraint and refinement—what's excluded is as important as what's 
included. Shot with 100mm lens for compression, f/11 for complete sharpness. 1:1 square 
format. The mood is calm, pure, sophisticated. Minimalist product photography aesthetic 
similar to high-end skincare brands.
```

### Configuration Notes
- **Resolution:** 4K (minimalism demands perfection)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 1:1 (balanced, centered)
- **Key Words:** "minimalist", "pure white", "centered", "negative space", "restrained", "clean"

## Example 6: Painted Artistic Style

### Scenario
Book cover or decorative art with traditional media feel.

### Prompt
```
Create an image that looks like an oil painting on canvas depicting a garden scene. 
A cobblestone path winds through an overgrown cottage garden bursting with flowers—
roses, hollyhocks, lavender, and wildflowers in vibrant pinks, purples, and yellows. 
A weathered wooden gate stands partially open in the midground. Dappled sunlight filters 
through tree branches above. The painting technique shows visible brushstrokes—thick 
impasto texture in the flower areas, loose impressionistic strokes for the foliage, 
more refined detail in the path and gate. The color palette is rich and saturated but 
maintains painterly quality rather than photographic precision. Colors blend and interact 
like wet oil paint—no hard digital edges. Subtle canvas texture visible throughout. The 
style combines impressionist color and light with enough representational detail to be 
readable. 4:5 portrait orientation. The mood is romantic, peaceful, idyllic. Traditional 
oil painting aesthetic with contemporary subject.
```

### Configuration Notes
- **Resolution:** 4K (canvas texture detail)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 (classic painting proportion)
- **Key Words:** "oil painting", "canvas", "brushstrokes", "impasto", "impressionistic", "painterly"

## Example 7: Neon Cyberpunk Aesthetic

### Scenario
Gaming or tech brand requiring futuristic urban style.

### Prompt
```
Generate a cyberpunk cityscape with intense neon aesthetic. A rain-slicked city street 
at night reflects brilliant neon signs in magenta, cyan, and electric blue. Towering 
buildings with holographic advertisements loom above. Steam rises from street grates. 
Figures in futuristic clothing with glowing accents walk past, slightly motion-blurred. 
Flying vehicles' light trails streak across the upper portion. The color palette is 
dominated by saturated neons against deep blacks—hot pinks, electric blues, acid greens, 
and vibrant purples. The lighting is entirely artificial: neon tubes, LED screens, 
holographic projections. The atmosphere is dense with moisture making all lights glow 
and bloom. Vertical lines dominate with towering architecture. The style combines photo-
realistic urban details with heightened color and futuristic elements. Shot with wide-angle 
lens, looking slightly upward. 9:16 vertical orientation. The mood is dystopian, electric, 
immersive. Cyberpunk 2077 and Blade Runner aesthetic with maximalist neon.
```

### Configuration Notes
- **Resolution:** 4K (neon glow detail)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (vertical, emphasize height)
- **Key Words:** "cyberpunk", "neon", "rain-slicked", "holographic", "saturated", "glow", "bloom"

## Example 8: Watercolor Illustration

### Scenario
Children's book or whimsical brand illustration.

### Prompt
```
Illustrate a whimsical forest scene in watercolor painting style. A small fox sits in 
a sunlit clearing surrounded by delicate wildflowers and mushrooms. The painting technique 
shows characteristic watercolor properties: transparent layers allowing white paper to 
show through for highlights, colors bleeding and blending at edges creating soft transitions, 
varied color intensity from diluted washes to concentrated pigment, natural water blooms 
and granulation texture, and loose organic edges rather than crisp lines. The color palette 
is gentle and harmonious—soft oranges and rusty reds for the fox, sage greens and olive 
for foliage, soft yellows for flowers, and lavender shadows. White of the paper provides 
luminosity. Visible paper texture throughout. Some areas detailed while others are loose 
suggesting forms with minimal brushstrokes. 3:4 portrait orientation. The mood is gentle, 
magical, storybook charm. Traditional watercolor illustration aesthetic.
```

### Configuration Notes
- **Resolution:** 4K (watercolor texture detail)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 3:4 (book illustration standard)
- **Key Words:** "watercolor", "transparent layers", "bleeding edges", "water blooms", "paper texture"

## Example 9: High Fashion Editorial

- **Aspect Ratio:** 3:4 (editorial standard)

## Color Grading Styles

### Warm & Nostalgic
```
Color palette: golden yellows, burnt oranges, warm browns, muted greens. 
Slightly faded saturation. Warm white balance (3000K-4000K). Soft contrast.
```

### Cool & Modern
```
Color palette: steel blues, cool grays, mint greens, white. Crisp saturation. 
Cool white balance (6000K-7000K). High contrast with deep blacks.
```

### Cinematic Teal & Orange
```
Shadows pushed toward teal/cyan (180°). Highlights pushed toward orange (30°). 
High saturation in key colors. Moderate contrast. Popular in blockbuster films.
```

### Faded Vintage
```
Reduced saturation across all colors. Lifted blacks (gray rather than true black). 
Warm yellow-green cast overall. Soft contrast with compressed highlights.
```

### High Contrast B&W
```
Pure black shadows, bright white highlights. High contrast. Can add subtle cool 
or warm tone. Emphasizes shape, form, texture over color.
```

### Pastel Dreamy
```
Soft, desaturated colors—baby blues, blush pinks, mint greens, lavender, cream. 
Light and airy with lifted blacks. Low contrast. Ethereal mood.
```
