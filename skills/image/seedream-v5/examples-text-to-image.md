## PHOTOREALISTIC PORTRAITS

### Example 1: Film Noir Detective — Close-Up (Shadows of Ashford)
```
Prompt: A weathered male detective in his late 40s with deep-set eyes and 
a three-day stubble, wearing a rumpled dark fedora tilted low. Close-up 
portrait from chest up, harsh side lighting from a desk lamp casting sharp 
shadows across half his face. Shot on ARRI Alexa LF with 85mm f/1.4 lens, 
shallow depth of field. Pushed Tri-X 400 film grain, high contrast black 
and white, noir aesthetic.

Variant: Seedream 5.0 Lite
Image Size: portrait_4_3
Seed: null

Director's Notes: Film stock reference (Tri-X 400) provides authentic noir grain 
and contrast. 85mm compression flatters facial features while maintaining dramatic 
shadow fall-off. Side lighting from desk lamp is classic noir.
```
