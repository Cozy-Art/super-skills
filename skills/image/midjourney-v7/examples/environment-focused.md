## Environment Mood Keywords

**Peaceful:** serene, tranquil, calm, quiet, gentle  
**Dramatic:** intense, powerful, striking, bold  
**Mysterious:** enigmatic, ethereal, foggy, shadowy  
**Energetic:** vibrant, dynamic, bustling, lively  
**Desolate:** abandoned, empty, lonely, barren  

---

## Pro Tips for Environments

1. **Specify time of day** - "golden hour," "midday," "blue hour," "night"
2. **Include weather** - "rain," "fog," "clear sky," "overcast"
3. **Describe light quality** - "harsh," "soft," "diffused," "dramatic"
4. **Set atmosphere** - "bustling," "abandoned," "serene," "chaotic"
5. **Use specific architectural styles** when relevant
6. **Consider aspect ratio** - 21:9 for epic landscapes, 9:16 for vertical scenes

---

**Remember:** Environment prompts should be 20-60 words. Focus on key visual elements: lighting, atmosphere, and distinctive features.
