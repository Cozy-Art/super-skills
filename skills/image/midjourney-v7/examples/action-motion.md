## Parameter Strategy for Action

### For Clean Frozen Action
```
--s 200-300 (moderate stylization)
--q 2 (extra detail for motion)
--ar 16:9 or 3:4 depending on orientation
```

### For Artistic Motion Blur
```
--s 350-500 (higher stylization)
--ar appropriate to scene
--c 20-30 for variation in motion capture
```

### For Epic Battle Scenes
```
--s 400-600 (high stylization for drama)
--ar 21:9 (ultrawide for epic scale)
--q 2 for detail
```

---

## Action Scene Composition

**Rule of Thirds:** Place subject at intersection points  
**Leading Lines:** Use environment to direct eye to action  
**Negative Space:** Leave space in direction of motion  
**Dynamic Angles:** Low angles = power, high angles = scale  

---

**Pro Tip:** For action scenes, use phrases like "mid-air," "peak moment," "frozen instant" to capture decisive moments. Midjourney v7 excels at understanding these temporal cues.
