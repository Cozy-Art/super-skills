# Mystic 2.5 Fluid - Examples

The Fluid variant excels at fast generation with smooth, consistent visuals perfect for cinematic frames and high-volume production.

**Key Features:**
- Fast generation speed
- Excellent consistency across sequences
- Smooth, cinematic visuals
- NO LoRA support
- Over-moderated (some words flagged)
- 80 credits/image

## MODERATION WARNING EXAMPLES

### Example 28: Avoiding Flagged Words
```
❌ BAD (may be flagged):
"Soldiers in combat during war"

✅ GOOD (alternative phrasing):
"Military personnel in historical reenactment scene"

Note: Fluid uses Google Imagen 3 backend with strict moderation
Words like "war", "combat", "violence" may be flagged
Use alternative phrasing for similar concepts
```

---

**Pro Tip for Fluid Variant:** Keep Creative Detailing at 33 (default) and never change it for sequences. Enable Fixed Generation and use the same seed across all shots in a series. This variant's superpower is **consistency** - perfect for campaigns, storyboards, and frames destined for video conversion. The smoothness and speed make it ideal for high-volume production work.
