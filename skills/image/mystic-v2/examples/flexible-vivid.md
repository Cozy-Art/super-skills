# Mystic 2.5 Flexible - Examples

The Flexible variant offers best prompt adherence with vivid, saturated colors perfect for illustrations, fantasy, and branding.

**Key Features:**
- Best prompt adherence in family
- Vivid, saturated HDR aesthetic
- Excellent for fantasy and illustrations
- NO LoRA support (cannot use Custom Characters/Styles)
- 80 credits/image
