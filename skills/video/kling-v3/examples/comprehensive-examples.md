# Kling 3.0 - Comprehensive Examples

All prompts follow the 5-layer structure: **Scene → Characters → Action → Camera → Audio & Style**

---

## CHARACTER-FOCUSED EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Character Introduction with Elements
```
Dimly lit detective office at night with venetian blinds casting shadows. A weathered 
detective in his 50s wearing a brown trench coat and fedora sits at cluttered desk, 
examining case files. First, he leans forward studying a photograph closely, then 
sets it down with a heavy sigh, finally leaning back with hands clasped behind head. 
Slow dolly-in on his face, shallow depth of field. Film noir aesthetic with harsh 
desk lamp creating dramatic shadows, film grain texture.

Negative: smiling, laughing, bright colors, morphing, disfigured hands
```
**Duration:** 10s | **Resolution:** 1080p | **Aspect:** 16:9  
**Elements:** Upload 2-3 character reference images | **Face Adherence:** 75

### Example 3: Multi-Shot Character Sequence (3.0 Omni)
```
**Shot 1 (5s):** Wide establishing shot. A space captain in sleek uniform enters 
the starship bridge, crew members at stations around her. She strides confidently 
toward the captain's chair. Slow dolly following her movement. Futuristic aesthetic 
with cool blue lighting from holographic displays.

**Shot 2 (6s):** Medium shot. The captain settles into chair as crew member approaches 
with data pad. She takes it, expression shifting to concern as she reads. @Captain says 
firmly, "How long until they arrive?" Clean handheld shot. Ambient ship hum, alert beep.

**Shot 3 (4s):** Close-up on captain's face as she makes decision, jaw set with 
determination. Rack focus from data pad to her eyes. @Captain commands, "Battle stations." 
Tense atmosphere, alert klaxon begins.

Negative: smiling, cheerful, cartoonish, bright colors, morphing
```
**Total:** 15s | **Elements:** 3 captain reference images | **Face Adherence:** 80

## TECHNICAL DEMONSTRATIONS

### Example 12: CFG Scale Comparison

**Low CFG (0.3) - More Creative:**
```
Bustling Tokyo street at night with neon signs, crowd of people walking. Camera 
drifts through scene observing details. Cyberpunk aesthetic.
```

**Medium CFG (0.5) - Balanced:**
```
Bustling Shibuya crossing in Tokyo at night, dense crowd crossing intersection, massive 
neon billboards and digital screens illuminating scene. First, camera reveals street 
level pedestrian perspective. Then it rises slowly showing scale of crowd. Finally, it 
reaches elevated position showing full intersection flow. Smooth crane shot ascending. 
Vibrant cyberpunk aesthetic with saturated neon colors, urban energy, light rain 
creating reflections.
```

**High CFG (0.8) - Strict Adherence:**
```
Exact recreation: Shibuya crossing, Tokyo, night, 8pm, heavy rain, crowd with umbrellas, 
specific neon signs (Starbucks, Gap, Forever 21) visible, scramble crossing pattern, 
109 building prominent, steam from street vents, traffic lights red. Static wide shot 
from elevated south perspective. Photorealistic aesthetic with accurate architectural 
detail, proper lighting physics, authentic crowd density.
```

### Example 13: Face Adherence Demonstration

**Face Adherence: 42 (Natural Variation)**
```
A businesswoman in office environment, different outfit from reference, natural expression 
variation, similar but not identical to source material.
```

**Face Adherence: 75 (Strong Consistency)**
```
The businesswoman maintains very close facial resemblance to reference images, outfit 
can vary, expressions closely match source, appropriate for multi-shot sequences 
requiring character continuity.
```

**Face Adherence: 100 (Exact Copy)**
```
Precise replication of reference character, exact facial features, minimal variation 
from source, best for scenes requiring absolutely identical appearance across all shots.
```

## MULTI-SHOT STORYBOARDS

### Example 14: Three-Act Micro-Narrative
```
**Shot 1 (5s):** Wide shot. A scientist in white lab coat enters high-tech laboratory, 
looking concerned at equipment readings on screens. She walks purposefully toward 
containment chamber. Slow dolly following her. Clean futuristic aesthetic with cool 
fluorescent lighting.

**Shot 2 (6s):** Medium shot. The scientist stops before sealed chamber containing 
glowing vial, hand hovering over control panel hesitantly. @Scientist says nervously, 
"This could change everything." Close handheld, slight shake. Equipment humming, 
ventilation system ambient.

**Shot 3 (4s):** Close-up on scientist's hand as she makes decision, pressing the 
activation button. Rack focus from button to vial inside chamber beginning to react. 
@Scientist whispers, "Here we go." Tense atmosphere. Alert beep, containment hiss.

Negative: smiling, cheerful, bright colors, cartoonish, morphing, extra fingers
```
**Total:** 15s | **Elements:** 3 scientist references | **Face Adherence:** 80

## QUICK TEMPLATES

### Character Introduction:
```
[Location and time]. [Character description with specific details]. [Sequential actions: 
first, then, finally]. [Camera movement]. [Lighting and style]. [@Character dialogue 
if needed]. [Audio details].

Negative: smiling, laughing, morphing, disfigured hands, extra fingers
```

### Action Sequence:
```
[Dynamic environment]. [Subject with distinctive appearance]. [Action progression: 
initial, transition, conclusion]. [Fast tracking/following camera]. [Cinematic style]. 
[Sound effects]. [Ambient audio].

Negative: robotic movement, unrealistic physics, static, morphing, low resolution
```

### Dialogue Scene:
```
[Indoor/outdoor setting with atmosphere]. [Two subjects described]. [Interaction 
sequence]. [Camera: two-shot or shot-reverse-shot]. @Character1: "[dialogue]." 
@Character2: "[dialogue]." [Lighting mood]. [Ambient sound].

Negative: smiling broadly, laughing, overly theatrical, cartoonish, morphing
```

---

**Pro Tip:** Always structure prompts in the 5-layer order, use negative prompts, and leverage Elements with 70-80 face adherence for multi-shot character consistency. Kling 3.0 rewards detailed, sequential descriptions.
