## CHARACTER WITH SUBJECT REFERENCE

### Example 1: Character Introduction
```
A young woman in her 20s with long dark hair and casual streetwear stands on 
a city rooftop at sunset, wind gently moving her hair. She gazes thoughtfully 
at the skyline with a peaceful, contemplative expression. [Slow dolly-in on 
her face]. Golden hour lighting, warm and serene mood.
```
**Duration:** 6s | **Resolution:** 1080P  
**Subject Reference:** Upload front-facing portrait of the woman

## MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE WORKFLOW

### Example 33: 18-Second Narrative (Three 6s Clips)

**Shot 1 (6s) - Establishing:**
```
[Subject Reference: Woman in business attire]

Wide shot of modern office building exterior at dawn. The woman exits through 
glass doors, adjusting her bag, looking determined. [Dolly back as she approaches]. 
Cool morning light, professional atmosphere.
```

**Shot 2 (6s) - Action:**
```
[Same Subject Reference]

The woman walks briskly through busy city sidewalk, checking her watch, weaving 
around pedestrians with purpose. [Tracking shot from side keeping pace]. Bright 
daylight, urban energy.
```

**Shot 3 (6s) - Reaction:**
```
[Same Subject Reference]

Close-up as she enters an important building, pausing at door. Deep breath, 
expression shifts from nervous to confident. She enters frame. [Medium close-up, 
slight dolly-in]. Interior lobby lighting, pivotal moment.
```

**Post-Production:** Edit three clips together for complete 18-second sequence

## Quick Templates

### Emotion Template:
```
[Subject with emotional state] [action showing emotion progression] in [environment]. 
[Camera movement]. [Lighting that enhances mood].
```

### Motion Template:
```
[Subject] [specific movement with physics detail] in [environment]. 
[Camera tracking movement]. [Atmospheric lighting].
```

### Image-to-Video Template:
```
[Upload reference image]

[Subject] [feasible action from pose], [detail of movement]. [Camera direction]. 
[Atmospheric evolution].
```

**Pro Tip:** Hailuo 2.3's sweet spot is 4-6 seconds with emphasis on natural motion, emotional nuance, and realistic physics. Use Subject Reference for character consistency, keep descriptions clear and natural, and chain clips in post for longer sequences.
